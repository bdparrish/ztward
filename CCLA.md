**Version 1.0**

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement (the "Agreement") lets an organization authorize its
employees to contribute to Ztward (the "Project"), maintained by Benjamin Parrish (the
"Maintainer"), on the organization's behalf. It complements the
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md), which each Designated Employee must also
accept.

The Corporation keeps the copyright in its Contributions. This Agreement gives the Maintainer a
license to use them, including under the Project's commercial license, as described in section 2.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Corporation"** means the legal entity named in Schedule A, together with every entity that
  controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with it. "Control" means direct or
  indirect ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities or other
  ownership interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management by contract or
  otherwise.
- **"Designated Employee"** means an employee or contractor of the Corporation listed in
  Schedule B, as updated under section 9.
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any changes or additions to
  existing work, that a Designated Employee intentionally Submits to the Project on behalf of the
  Corporation.
- **"Submit"** has the meaning given in the Individual Contributor License Agreement.
